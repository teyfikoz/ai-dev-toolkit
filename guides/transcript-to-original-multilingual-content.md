# From video research to original multilingual content

Verify each source video's own view count and record the observation date. Channel subscribers or total channel views are not a substitute. Keep source metadata and transcript acquisition status separate: native captions, creator-published script, unavailable, or blocked. A failed translated subtitle request does not invalidate an already downloaded original-language caption file.

Use transcripts to identify audience questions and explanatory patterns. Write a new scenario, example, structure, and visual treatment. Translating a copyrighted script closely or replacing the voice is not original authorship. Do not redistribute complete research transcripts in a public repository. Source attribution does not itself grant reuse permission.

For psychology-related content, distinguish historical theories from supported clinical claims. Do not present an unverified quotation as authentic, diagnose viewers or other people, promise emotional invulnerability, or blame people for being harmed. Educational self-reflection must not impersonate therapy.

Generate a small language pilot before expanding. Application language availability does not prove script, narration, typography, or pronunciation quality. Local synthesis can reduce generation costs, but human language review remains a separate status. Review line wrapping, glyph coverage, timing, full video decode, narration clarity, and music level.

Keep a manifest containing source URL, source observation date, original script, language, media hash, caption, exact hashtag count, review status, music source, and actual publication permalink. Mark preparation and publication separately.

If native Instagram audio is required, an embedded local preview score does not satisfy that requirement. Keep a narration-only master, select a sound available to the actual account, check usage rights and the final mix, and verify the published result. Do not label a sound or hashtag as currently trending without current evidence. Neither music nor hashtags guarantee virality.

An account-specific music requirement must also be enforced by any automation queue. An absent audio selection is a held item, even when the preview file already contains music. Never report an automation service's healthy state as evidence that an Instagram post was published.
